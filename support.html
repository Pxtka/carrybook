Frequently asked questions

Pro is not showing on my new phone?
Open Settings and choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you purchased with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions.

Where are my distances stored?
Only on your phone. They move to a new phone with your phone's backup. You can also export your own backup (Settings → Export backup) and restore it on another phone with Settings → Import.

Can I use the app in competitions?
Turn on competition mode in course mode: conditions and the club finder are switched off. Your own measured distances and the printed yardage card are allowed (Rule 4.3).
