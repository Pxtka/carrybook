Privacy Policy

Effective from 21 October 2026

In short: Carrybook stores your club distances only on your phone. The app has no user accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Location is used only to fetch the weather and is not stored.

2. What data is processed

Data stored on your phone. Your clubs, distances, shots, detailed data (such as ball speed and spin), bags, balls and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else. They may be included in your phone's own backups (iCloud or Google), which you control.

Imported files. When you import a launch monitor CSV file, it is read and processed on your phone. The file is not sent anywhere.

Backup. If you export a backup (Settings → Export backup), the file is saved wherever you choose in your phone's share sheet, such as iCloud Drive or email. Carrybook never sends the file anywhere itself. The file contains no Pro subscription details.

Location (optional). If you allow location access, the app fetches current weather and your course's altitude based on your location. Before sending, the location is rounded to about one kilometer and passed through our relay server to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway). The relay server does not permanently store your location or IP address, and MET Norway does not see your IP address. The compass uses your phone's sensor and heading data is not sent.

Purchases. Payments for the Pro subscription are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or bank details. To check your subscription status we use RevenueCat, which receives a random, anonymous user identifier, purchase details (product, time, validity, country and currency) and basic device and app version information. The identifier is not linked to your name or email.

Contacting us. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to handle your request.

4. Recipients and transfers

  • Apple and Google process purchases under their own privacy policies.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) processes purchase data on our behalf.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) operates the relay server.
  • MET Norway (Norway, EEA) receives the rounded location for weather data.

Transfers to the United States rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell data or share it for marketing.

5. Retention

Data on your phone is kept until you delete it in the app (Settings → Reset all data) or remove the app. Location is not retained. Purchase records are kept as long as accounting and consumer protection laws require. Emails are deleted once the matter is resolved and they are no longer needed.

6. Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access and rectify your data, to request erasure, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. Because your distances stay on your phone, you can manage them yourself in the app. For requests about purchase data, contact carrybook.app@icloud.com and include the Support ID shown in the app under Settings → About.

You can lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi/en) or your local supervisory authority.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

We update this policy if the app's data processing changes. We will announce significant changes in the app or its update notes.
